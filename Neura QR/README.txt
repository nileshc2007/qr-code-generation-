CAMPUS TREASURE HUNT WEBSITE

1. Upload index.html, style.css and script.js to any static website host.
2. Your site will look like:
   https://your-site.example/?clue=1
   https://your-site.example/?clue=2
   https://your-site.example/?clue=3

3. The QR images in qr_codes are placeholders because the final public URL is not known yet.
4. After you have your public site URL, replace the QR destinations with:
   URL/?clue=1
   URL/?clue=2
   URL/?clue=3

5. Replace the sample clues in script.js with your actual clues.
